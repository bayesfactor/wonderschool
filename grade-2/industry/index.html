Making Things

Go and look at the zipper on your coat. Turn the little pull-tab over. If there are three letters stamped on the back — YKK — then a company in Japan made it, along with roughly four out of every ten zippers on Earth.

Start here
Six Making Stations

A treasure hunt round your own house, a hundred people and their hundred jobs, company names that turn out to be picture-words, and a factory of your own to design.

 With everyone else
The Cabinet Meeting

You are the Inventor. Nobody gets to spend a single yen until you have told them what it is actually for.

The surprise in this lesson

The biggest company in Japan is one you have never heard of. Not Toyota. Not Nintendo. A shop called AEON, which employs 427,230 people — more than any other Japanese company in the world. Famous and big turn out to be two completely different things.

What you will need

A screen, plenty of plain paper, and colored pencils. Nothing to print. You will also need permission to wander round the house looking at labels.
