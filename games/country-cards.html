Games · for 2 to 5 players

Country Cards

The 100 largest countries on Earth, one shared device, four categories. Call a category, highest number wins the pile. There is no best card — and beating everyone means working out which category your country wins.

New game

How it works

  1. The 100 country cards are shuffled and dealt evenly to every player, face down.
  2. On your turn you see only your own top card. Pick the category you think will win — People, Land, Economy or Wealth.
  3. Everyone's top card is revealed. Highest number wins all the cards played.
  4. An exact tie sends the cards to the middle. Whoever wins the next round takes those too.
  5. The winner calls the next category. When the round limit is up, or one player holds every card, the biggest pile wins.

One device, passed around. Hand the phone or laptop to whoever's turn it is. You only ever see your own top card until the reveal — so choosing well is the whole skill.

100 most populous countries, rounded public estimates. Population: UN World Population Prospects, 2024 revision. Economy: nominal GDP, IMF World Economic Outlook, 2025. Wealth is that economy divided by that population (GDP per person). Land area: total area, land plus inland water. Figures are rounded for play and the exact edge of the "top 100" shifts a little between sources — treat every number as an estimate, which is exactly how the sixth-grade data desk teaches you to treat them.
