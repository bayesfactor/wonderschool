The Japan Desk

You are the Illustrator. Nothing goes on the wall chart unless you draw it — and you decide how much room everyone else gets.

What you will be able to do

Name Japan's four big islands. Read six kanji — real ones, the same ones Japanese children learn. Name three things invented in Japan. And write a haiku with exactly the right number of beats.

You will need: the printed Explorer Sheet, a pencil, colored pencils, and a few sheets of plain paper — for your drawings and for the origami cup at the end. It really holds water. Test it.
