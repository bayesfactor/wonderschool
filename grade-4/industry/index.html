The Factory Cup

Twelve contests. Japan against China, the United States, Germany and South Korea. Every one is a real number from a real organisation. You predict the winner before you see it — and the score you care about at the end is not yours.

The game
Twelve Contests

Cars, robots, chips, machines, money. Pick who you think wins, then watch the bars come in. Points for being right, extra for a streak.

 With everyone else
The Cabinet Meeting

You are the Accountant. One trillion yen, four people who all want it, and you are the only one who can say what it buys.

The thing to watch for

Japan does not win many of these twelve. That is not the interesting part. The interesting part is which ones it does win — and what those have in common. Get to the end before you decide what you think.

Nothing to print

All twelve contests run on a screen. Bring paper if you want to keep your own score.
