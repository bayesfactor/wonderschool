Fourth grade · lesson 2

Power Japan

You get twenty power stations and one country of 123 million people. You cannot make it cheap, safe and clean all at once — nobody on Earth has managed it. Pick your compromise. Then history happens to you, seven times, with real dates.

The game
The Control Room

Place twenty power stations. Watch three dials. Then draw an event card — a tsunami, a war, a heatwave — and find out whether the lights stay on.

 Then this
Power Source Cards

Nine sources, six statistics each. Sort them any way you like and see who wins — then notice that the winner changes depending on what you decided to care about.

 With everyone else
The Energy Meeting

You are the Engineer. You are the only one at the table who knows whether the plan actually works, and everyone has to go through you.

The rule of the game

Japan's government has an official name for this problem: 3E + S. Energy security, Economic efficiency, Environment — plus Safety. They write all four down together so that nobody can quietly forget one. You are about to find out why they had to.

One number to beat

Japan makes about 16 out of every 100 units of energy it uses. The rest arrives by ship. Every country in the world would like that number higher. Watch what happens to yours.
