Japan: The Paradox File

Japan has the 4th-largest economy on Earth and ranks 39th in money per person. It scores 96 out of 100 on political freedom and 30th of 176 on economic freedom. Its own government says 70% of Japanese are Shinto and 70% are Buddhist; surveys say 70% have no religion. Every one of those numbers is correct.

The one that catches everybody

Every reference book says Japan's literacy rate is 99%. UNESCO — whose job this is — publishes no literacy figure for Japan at all, nor for the United States, Germany, the UK, France, Canada or Australia. Japan's last real national literacy survey was in 1948. The 99% is a gap that secondary sources filled in, copied from each other, and hardened into a fact. Case File 05.

The rule for this lesson: a number without a source and a year is a rumour. Every figure in the dossier and the Data Desk carries both. Check them.
