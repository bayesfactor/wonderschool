Japan

For thirty-five years Japan said it had 6,852 islands. In 2023 they counted again with better maps and found 14,125. A whole country had lost track of seven thousand pieces of itself.

Start here
Japan Discovery Passport

Six stations, six stamps. Islands, Mount Fuji, picture-words, games, the red gate, and a poem. Read it on screen or print it as a booklet.

 With everyone else
The Japan Desk

You are the Illustrator. Nothing goes on the wall chart unless you draw it.

What you will be able to do

Name Japan's four big islands. Read six kanji — real ones, the same ones Japanese children learn. Name three things invented in Japan. And write a haiku with exactly the right number of beats.

You will need: a pencil, colored pencils, and one extra sheet of plain paper for the origami cup at the end. It really holds water. Test it.
