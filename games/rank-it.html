Games · 1 to 5 players

Rank It

Four countries, one measure. Drag them into order, highest to lowest. The closer you get, the more you score — get every pair the right way round for a perfect round. Play solo, or pass the device around a table of up to five.

How it works

  1. Each round shows four random countries and one measure — people, land, economy, wealth, inequality or a freedom score.
  2. Put them in order, most at the top down to least. Drag the cards, or use the ▲▼ buttons.
  3. Score is by pairs in the right order: there are six pairs among four countries, and you earn points for each one you get the right way round. All six right is a perfect round.
  4. With 2–5 players everyone gets the same four countries and measure and takes a turn. The cards are re-shuffled before each turn, so you can't see how anyone else answered. Highest total after eight rounds wins.

No best answer to memorise. The measures pull in different directions — the country with the most people is rarely the richest per person — so each round is its own puzzle. Same data as Country Cards.

Pass the device over, then tap to see your four countries — freshly shuffled, so no peeking at anyone else's order.
