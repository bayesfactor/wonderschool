Country Cards

The 100 largest countries on Earth, dealt as a deck with their flags. Call a category — People, Land, Economy, Wealth, Inequality or a freedom score — and the highest number takes the pile. There is no best card, and finding that out is how you win.

Grows from the fourth-grade Japan card game

Rank It

Four countries, one measure. Drag them into order, highest to lowest. The closer you get, the more you score — get every pair the right way round for a perfect round. Eight rounds, then a rank.

How these differ from the classroom versions

The lessons each carry a paper game you cut out and play by hand. The versions here are the same games made for a screen and more players — they shuffle, deal, keep score and settle ties for you, so the table can spend its time arguing about which category to call rather than adding up cards.
