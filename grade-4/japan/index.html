Japan

Japan is 4th in the world by economy, 11th by population, 62nd by land area and 30th by economic freedom. All four are true at the same time. What a country's rank is depends entirely on what you decide to count.

Country Cards

Twelve cards to cut out. Call a category, highest number wins. There is no best card — that is the whole point.

 With everyone else
The Japan Desk

You are the Quartermaster. You run the deck, and the sixth grader cannot write a word until you have delivered four approved numbers.

What you will be able to do

Say Japan's population, area and economy with their world ranks. Explain why a country can be 4th in total money and 39th in money per person. Explain why nobody in Japan votes for the Prime Minister. And name four Japanese inventions that are in your house right now.

Boss round. Pick one thing Japan invented and write three sentences: what it is, what people did before it existed, and what would break tomorrow if it vanished. Hard mode: pick one nobody else would think of.
