Rank It

Four countries, one measure. Drag them into order, highest to lowest. The closer you get, the more you score — get every pair the right way round for a perfect round.

How it works

  1. Each round you get four random countries and one measure — people, land, economy, wealth, inequality or a freedom score.
  2. Put them in order, most at the top down to least. Drag the cards, or use the ▲▼ buttons.
  3. Score is by pairs in the right order: there are six pairs among four countries, and you earn points for each one you get the right way round. All six right is a perfect round.
  4. Eight rounds, then a final score and a rank. Every card shows its real number when the round is graded, so you learn as you go.

No best answer to memorise. The measures pull in different directions — the country with the most people is rarely the richest per person — so each round is its own puzzle. Same idea as Country Cards, turned into a solo challenge.
