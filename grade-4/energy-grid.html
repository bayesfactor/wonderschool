The Control Room

Twenty power stations. One country. Three dials that fight each other.

Step 1 — Build the country

Place all twenty stations. Each one is a different kind of machine, and they are not equally useful — a solar farm only works while the sun is up, so twenty solar farms would leave Japan in the dark every single night.

Carbon figures are lifetime medians from the IPCC, Annex III. Japan's grid was 477 g/kWh in 2025 — Ember. “Cheap” and the availability factors are simplified game scores, close to real ones but not exact prices.

Step 3 — History happens

Seven cards. Every one of them really happened, or really could. Draw them one at a time. You may rebuild between cards — that is allowed, and it is exactly what real ministers do.

Seven cards survived

The challenge

Get through all seven cards with the lights on every time — and finish under 300 grams of CO₂ with at least 40% made in Japan.

It can be done. It is harder than it looks, and every answer that works makes somebody unhappy about something.

Three things worth noticing while you fail:

  • Twenty solar farms survive none of the seven cards. Not one.
  • Twenty coal stations survive all seven — at 820 grams and nothing made at home. Winning the wrong game is easy.
  • Load “Copy France”, then draw the tsunami card. That single card is the whole argument Japan has been having with itself since 2011.

If you manage all seven cards, under 300 grams, and over 50% made in Japan — go and tell your teacher, because that one is genuinely hard.
